# Unintentional interaction with my cars unconventional CD player
Radios have been a staple in automobiles since the 1920's with some of the first car radios simply being house radios modified to fit into cars anyway they could. Since car radios have been around for such a long time, this has allowed for the evolution of two different types of car radios, such as simple car radios like the one shown in figure 1 that are equipped with a CD player, a knob for volume, a small screen for letting you know what radio mode you are in, and several buttons for functions such as changing the radio station or ejecting a CD.
