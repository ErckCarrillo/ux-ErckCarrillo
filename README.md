# Unintentional interaction with my cars unconventional CD player
Radios have been a staple in automobiles since the 1920's with some of the first car radios simply being house radios modified to fit into cars anyway they could. Since car radios have been around for such a long time, this has allowed for the evolution of two different types of car radios, with the first being older style car radios like the one shown in figure 1 that are equipped with a CD player, a knob for volume, a small screen for letting you know what radio mode you are in, and several buttons for functions such as changing the radio station or ejecting a CD. While the second type of car radio, which is more often seen in newer cars, is the touchscreen "infotainment" systems which include all the features of the older style radios in a much more sleek and modern looking package
with much less buttons and include additional features such as the integration of GPS navigation and hands free calling and texting. This type of radio can be seen in figure 2.
